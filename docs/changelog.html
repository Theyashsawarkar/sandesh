Changelog

Every change to sandesh, newest first. Releases are git tags (all tags, GitHub releases); the source of this page is CHANGELOG.md.

Unreleased

  • Reply to a specific chat message: Enter (or 2) on a chat lets j/k pick messages; r replies to the picked one, quoting it like Google Chat (quotedMessageMetadata), with "↩ replying to …" over the message box; from the list r answers the latest message from someone else. y copies and v saves the picked message.
  • Quoted replies show: a message that quotes another shows it above (❝ You: …); recent chats are fetched again once to fill this in.
  • No more "reply in a thread" labels: Google reports every space as threaded, and replies show inline as quotes, as in the browser.
  • Name a group: starting a chat with two or more people asks for a name (named: a space; unnamed: a group chat), and n names an existing group chat on Google Chat (it becomes a space, same people and messages).
  • Sign in again without leaving: when Google wants the newer permission (starting chats, naming groups), the window says so and Enter signs in again in the browser, then carries on with what you were doing.
  • Start a conversation (N in chat): pick people (from chat, mail contacts and the Workspace directory); sandesh opens the DM you already have with them (found on Google, earlier messages fetched) or starts one, a group chat for several, and puts you in the message box.
  • Chat search finds people and conversations: CONVERSATIONS, PEOPLE and MESSAGES groups; Enter on a person opens (or starts) your chat with them.
  • MCP chat_send takes to (email addresses) instead of a space.
  • Scheduled tabs: scheduled mail has its own Scheduled section next to Sent, and scheduled chat messages a Scheduled tab in chat, soonest first with their time and counts on the tabs (t moves, Enter edits, z cancels). Sent goes back to mail that went (and what's sending or failed).
  • Send later: Ctrl+t in a mail, t on review, Ctrl+t while typing a chat message: pick a time or type it ("tomorrow 9am", "fri 6pm", "in 2h", "3 nov 10:30"). Scheduled sends wait in the outbox and the background service sends them on time (on its own loop now, so a slow sync never delays one); a send that goes out late because the computer was off says so. They're listed on top of Sent (t moves, Enter edits, z cancels). CLI --at and outbox reschedule; MCP send_at.
  • Search no longer freezes the screen: a one-letter search took ~5 s on a real mailbox (the full-text index was queried once per thread); it is now one lookup (~20-50 ms), and it runs in the background after a short pause in typing, with stale answers dropped and Gmail's search cancelled when you type on. Previews are drawn in the background, once.
  • Search where you are: a mail section searches that section (Today, Sent, Inbox...), a chat space searches that space; Tab widens to all mail or every chat. Opening a chat result scrolls to the message and marks it.
  • ? is a window: every key, grouped in columns in the middle of the screen, with a filter as you type.
  • A file picker of its own (replacing fzf): a window over the screen with fuzzy matching and a preview of the highlighted file (text, image size or the picture itself with chafa, a PDF's first page with pdftotext); Tab picks several; Ctrl+r switches folder, Ctrl+h hidden.
  • Rename a chat (n): spaces on Google Chat (new chat.spaces permission; sign in again once), DMs and unnamed groups by a name kept on this machine.
  • The popup is bigger by default (96% × 94%).
  • Round ends on every filled label: @mentions in chat, the active tab, the mode label, key hints, the send button and the approval banner use Nerd Font half circles like the avatars (nerd_font = false keeps square).
  • A search window: / (or s) opens a floating search over the screen. Mail and chat from this machine show as you type, matches highlighted, with a preview of the highlighted result; when typing pauses, Gmail's own search adds what isn't here yet (☁). Gmail search syntax works. Replaces the old local filter and separate server search.
  • Older mail on demand: no background download by default any more (keep = "none"; it competed with searches for Gmail's rate limit). j at the end of a list loads the next 10 older threads of that section; + / - change the batch (10, 25, 50, 100), older_page the default.
  • Search results survive a refresh: they're kept like a section.
  • Rounded everywhere, round avatars: the selected card keeps rounded corners (heavier sides mark it), mail tables have rounded corners, and initials sit in round pills (Nerd Font half circles; nerd_font = false for plain ones).
  • Compose, redone (from the approved mockup): a new mail gets the whole screen; a reply keeps the original on the left (PgUp/PgDn). Recipients are pills with initials (←/→ selects, Backspace removes); someone you've never written to is marked first time. Suggestions highlight the typed letters and say why they're there (wrote 4× · 2d ago, wrote you 9×, directory). Cc/Bcc hidden until Ctrl+b. Your Gmail signature is fetched and added (Ctrl+g toggles). Drafts are saved on disk every few seconds and reopened by the same key. A status line with recipients, files, words and buttons; Ctrl+f switches the sending account. Tab now adds a typed contact and moves on.
  • A Sent section: who you wrote to, and whether they answered (↩ Priya replied 09:58, no reply yet · 2d); mail in the outbox (sending, waiting for approval, failed) on top, z cancels, R retries a failed send.
  • All your mail, not just 30 days: after the first sync, older mail is downloaded in the background (~150 threads a minute, newest first) until the mailbox is complete, with a progress bar in the top bar. keep limits it ("1y", "6m", "none").
  • A compose screen: c, r, a, f and D open a form in the right panel instead of a bare file in the editor: From, To, Cc, Bcc as chips, Subject, Files and the body, with a reply's quote folded underneath. Recipients autocomplete from your contacts (ranked by how often you write to them) and from your Google Workspace directory (People API). Ctrl+s sends, Ctrl+d saves a draft, Ctrl+o attaches, Ctrl+e still takes the whole mail to your editor and back; compose = "editor" keeps the old way. An agent's message is edited here too.
  • Syncs ~10× cheaper: a sync with nothing new went from 23 Google calls (12.6 s) to 2 (1.1 s) on a real account. Lookups that fail (a DM's title, a person's name) are remembered and retried every 6 hours instead of on every sync; changed spaces are fetched four at a time; cached spaces are read in one query. The background service has a soft 128 MB memory cap.
  • Chat read state follows the browser: a space read on the web or phone turns read here too, without waiting for a new message. M on a space is Chat's own "mark as unread" (it stays unread after a sync, and marks the latest message from someone else, even when you wrote last).
  • Forwarding keeps attachments: they're listed as Attach: original: … in the editor (delete to drop) and fetched from Gmail when sent.
  • File picker (+, U) starts in the folder you're in; the review screen shows each file's name, size and folder, and says when one is missing.
  • Fixes: W no longer glues the name onto the search (an empty name keeps the search); mail archive and friends say what they did; the outbox names files; sandesh mcp exits quietly when its client closes it; the setup screen's border no longer shifts on the title line.
  • SANDESH_DEBUG_HTTP=1 logs every Google request with its time.
  • Compose works without $EDITOR: with no editor set, sandesh used vi, which many systems don't have, so c, r and friends failed at once. It now picks the first installed of nvim, vim, vi, helix, micro, nano, kak and emacs, and says which editor failed and how to set one.
  • Chat sends instantly: chat messages skip the 10-second undo window (chat_undo_seconds, default 0; mail keeps undo_seconds), appear in the conversation as soon as Chat accepts them, and sending always jumps to the end of the chat.
  • The tmux popup runs the current sandesh: tmux's environment often lacks ~/.local/bin, so the plugin fell back to a copy built once in its own folder and kept running it after updates. It now looks in ~/.local/bin and ~/go/bin too, and rebuilds its own copy whenever the code is newer.
  • New chat messages show up: a conversation scrolled to the bottom stays at the bottom when messages arrive, like a chat app; before, they landed below the visible part. The space list says "You:" for your own last message.
  • Files, both ways: v saves a mail's attachments or a chat's files to ~/Downloads/sandesh (an fzf picker when there are several), V saves and opens them. Send files with Attach: in the mail editor, + on the review screen, or U in a chat space; --attach on mail send and chat send; mail save / chat save download from the shell. Big mails (over 4 MB) go through Gmail's upload endpoint. Files are copied privately when queued, so what you reviewed is what is sent.
  • Agents and files: mail_send, mail_draft and chat_send take attachments; such sends always ask for approval, which lists each file. New attachment_save tool, confined to the downloads folder.
  • Other people's chat messages get a solid bar in their colour.
  • Follows the desktop theme live: colours come from varna's palette.json (sandesh's own pick under tools.sandesh if the theme has one) and change on the running screen when the theme changes; [colors] in the config still win. SANDESH_NO_VARNA=1 turns it off.
  • Cleaner borders: panels and frames use the border colour instead of one almost the same as the background; the selected card is a heavy accent frame instead of a background fill, which looked muddy on translucent terminals.
  • No red error when Chat has no app configured: marking a space read works on this machine, and a quiet line says what to set up; messages that were cached empty are fetched again.
  • Syncs in the background, not when you open it: the screen shows the local copy at once and leaves syncing to the background service (setup now installs it); it syncs by itself only when the service isn't running. After a send, an archive or another change it syncs again shortly so the copy matches Google. Only one process syncs an account at a time.
  • Never hits Gmail's rate limit: requests are paced (40 quota units a second), Google's "slow down" answers are retried with backoff, and a first sync cut short resumes instead of starting over. The first sync takes the newest 150 threads (max_threads); older mail comes through search.
  • Real names in chat: names come from Chat's own messages, sandesh finds which member is you from your DMs (no People API needed), DMs are named after the other person and groups "Naeem, Rahul, Rugved +2"; placeholder "user 1234" names are no longer stored.
  • Chat is compact: one name line per group, a coloured bar per message, no boxes; messages that are only an attachment or a card say so.
  • Errors get their own line (Esc hides it) instead of covering the keys, and the common Google errors say what to do. Card borders are brighter (new border colour).
  • A new look: the mail list is one bordered card per thread with gaps between them; the thread view puts each message in its own card with a From / To / Date header and renders HTML mail as terminal formatting (headings, bold, lists, tables, buttons, numbered links), folding older messages and quoted replies (E, Q, o opens a link). Chat reads like a conversation: grouped by sender, yours on the right, day separators, thread replies and mentions marked; i types a message in place.
  • m marks read and M marks unread (no more toggle), for the thread or every selected one, in mail and chat.
  • Setup asks for the client file's path (with completion) instead of looking in Downloads, copies it into its config, and shows the three steps with a progress bar for the first sync.
  • Chat works without the People API: names fall back to "user 1234" instead of the whole chat sync failing.
  • The local copy keeps each message's HTML; existing copies refetch once.
  • Setup screen: opening sandesh (or prefix e) with no credentials no longer stops with an error. It says what's missing and takes it right there: the client file found in ~/Downloads, a path or pasted JSON, or a typed client ID and secret; then the Google sign-in in your browser (work account or personal Gmail) and the first sync, straight into the inbox. d tries the demo instead.

v0.1.02026-10-26release ↗

First release.

  • The screen: Gmail and Google Chat in one keyboard-driven terminal UI, laid out like sangam: sections on top (Inbox, Today, Unread, Starred, saved Gmail searches; All, Unread, DMs, Spaces for chat), the list on the left, the thread or space on the right. Vim keys, ? for all of them.
  • Write in your editor: compose, reply, reply all, forward and chat messages open $EDITOR; a review step shows exactly what goes out, then y sends, d saves a Gmail draft.
  • Undo: z undoes archive, trash, read and star, and cancels a send in its 10-second undo window.
  • Background sync (sandesh service install): Gmail's change feed and Chat's active spaces into a local SQLite copy with full-text search; desktop notifications for new inbox mail, DMs and mentions.
  • MCP server (sandesh mcp) with 11 tools for Claude Code and other agents. Agents' sends wait for your approval in a tmux popup, A in the screen or sandesh approve; mail content reaches them marked untrusted; send_policy sets how much they may send alone.
  • Claude keys in the screen: @ ask, S summarise, D draft a reply, T today's digest, through claude -p.
  • Command line for everything, with --json: mail, chat, status, sync, outbox, approve, auth, doctor, demo.
  • tmux plugin: prefix e popup and #{sandesh_status} for the status line.
